**What do you measure your short-term trading portfolio against?**
PowerTradingLab publishes free daily benchmarks for European short-term power trading. The buy-or-sell decision for each interval is published before the day-ahead auction, scored after delivery, and never changed afterwards.

**Where do the benchmarks come from?**
The benchmarks grew out of a six-year study, [*A Retrospective Look at 2020–2026 Short-Term Power Trading Opportunities in Europe*](/docs/chuchueva_2026_europe_power_trade_opportunities_2020_2026_eng.pdf) by [Irina Chuchueva](/chuchueva/). The report shows which bidding zones held "speculative fat" and how it decayed over time.
